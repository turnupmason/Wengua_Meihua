Purchases and subscriptions

Restore a purchase on a new phone

Sign in with the same account (Apple, Google or email), then open Membership and tap Restore purchases. Readings left to open are kept with your account and come back when you sign in.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Wengua and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.

Ask for a refund

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account and readings

Delete your account

In the app, open Me and tap Delete account. Your account and the purchase records kept with it are removed. Cancel any subscription with Apple separately.

Where are my readings kept?

Only on your phone. They are not uploaded, so deleting the app or its records removes them for good, and they do not move to a new phone.

A question says “coming soon”

Its judgments are still being written from the classical texts. You can cast and look at the hexagrams; such casts do not count toward the day’s readings.

购买与订阅

换手机后找回购买

用原来的账号（Apple、Google 或邮箱）登录，打开「会员」，点恢复购买。剩余的解锁次数跟着账号走，登录后自动回来。

取消订阅

订阅由 Apple 管理：在 iPhone 上打开设置 → 你的名字 → 订阅，选「问卦」，点取消订阅。请在本期结束前 24 小时以上取消，以免自动续费。

申请退款

App Store 的退款由 Apple 处理，请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

账号与问事记录

删除账号

在应用里打开我的，点删除账号，账号和其中的购买记录会一并删除。会员订阅请另到 Apple 账户中取消。

问事记录存在哪里？

只存在你的手机上，不上传。所以删除应用或删除记录后无法找回，换手机也不会带过去。

有的问题显示「古籍考据编写中·即将上线」

这一问的断语还在按古籍逐篇编写。现在可以先起卦看卦象，不计入当日次数。
