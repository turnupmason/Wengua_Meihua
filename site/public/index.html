问卦

梅花易数 · WENGUA

An iPhone app for reading the I Ching by the Plum Blossom method. Choose what you are asking about, cast by the hour, and read three hexagrams — the present, the course, the outcome — with a judgment written from the classical texts.

心有所问，卦有所解。择一事而问，按时起卦，本卦、互卦、变卦并排而观，断语依古籍写定。

Coming to the App Store · 即将上架
From the classics · 依古籍

Every judgment follows the Body–Use (体用) method of the Plum Blossom texts, worked out for each hexagram, moving line and month.

Checked by people · 人工审核

Each judgment is written in advance and reviewed by hand. Nothing is made up on the spot.

Yours alone · 只在本机

What you ask and the readings you get stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

Wengua is a tool for studying a classical tradition. Its readings are for reflection and are not a guarantee about real events, nor medical, legal or financial advice.
