问卦 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

「问卦 · 梅花易数」（下称“本应用”）由 Wengua Labs LLC 提供。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、怎么用、存在哪里，以及你怎样删除它们。

一、你的问事记录只在你的手机上

你所问的事、起出的卦、断语、问事录、收藏和应期回访，都只保存在你的设备上，不上传到我们的服务器。删除应用或在应用里删除记录，这些内容就随之删除。

二、登录时我们保存的信息

  • 用 Apple 登录：Apple 提供的账号标识，以及你同意提供的邮箱（可能是 Apple 的隐藏转发邮箱）和姓名。
  • 用 Google 登录：Google 提供的账号标识、邮箱和姓名。
  • 用邮箱登录：你的邮箱地址，用来发送验证码。
  • 不登录（游客或本机道号）：系统会生成一个匿名账号标识，只用来记录你的购买。

这些信息只用于登录、保存会员与购买，以及在你换手机后找回它们。

三、购买

会员订阅和解锁次数通过 Apple 的 App 内购买完成，付款由 Apple 处理，我们拿不到你的银行卡或支付信息。我们通过 RevenueCat 核对购买，服务器上保存你的账号标识、购买记录、剩余解锁次数，以及哪些卦已用次数解锁（只存一个随机编号，不含问事内容）。

四、我们使用的服务

  • Supabase：账号与解锁次数的存储（服务器位于美国）。
  • RevenueCat：App 内购买与订阅的核对。
  • Apple、Google：你选择用它们登录时的身份验证。

我们不投放广告，不追踪你在其他应用或网站上的行为，也不出售任何个人信息。

五、删除账号

在应用「我的」页面点「删除账号」，即可删除账号以及服务器上与之相关的购买与解锁记录。会员订阅由 Apple 管理，请另到 Apple 账户设置的「订阅」中取消。

六、未成年人

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。

七、变更与联系

政策如有变更，会在本页更新并注明日期。有任何问题，请写信至 support@wengualabs.com。

Wengua Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Wengua (“the app”) is provided by Wengua Labs LLC. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, where it is kept, and how you can delete it.

1. Your readings stay on your phone

The matters you ask about, the hexagrams cast, their judgments, your history, saved readings and follow-ups are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting them in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

2. What we keep when you sign in

  • Sign in with Apple: your Apple account identifier and, if you choose to share them, your email (possibly Apple’s private relay address) and name.
  • Sign in with Google: your Google account identifier, email and name.
  • Email sign-in: your email address, used to send sign-in codes.
  • Without signing in (guest): an anonymous account identifier, used only to record purchases.

We use this only to sign you in, keep your membership and purchases, and restore them on a new phone.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and reading credits are bought through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment; we never see your card or payment details. We confirm purchases through RevenueCat. Our server keeps your account identifier, your purchase records, the credits you have left, and which readings you opened with a credit (stored as a random number only, never the content of your question).

4. Services we use

  • Supabase: account and credit storage (servers in the United States).
  • RevenueCat: confirming in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple and Google: verifying your identity when you sign in with them.

We show no ads, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell personal information.

5. Deleting your account

Tap “Delete account” on the Me page to delete your account and the purchase and credit records kept with it. Subscriptions are managed by Apple; cancel them under Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: support@wengualabs.com.
